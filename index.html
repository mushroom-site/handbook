Questions, answered

Is this really for beginners?

Yes. It starts with choosing a growing space and a first oyster mushroom strain, and walks through every stage in order.

Is it written for Nigerian conditions?

Yes. It covers coastal humidity, harmattan, rainy season, unstable power and water, with advice by region.

Do I need expensive equipment?

No. It uses oyster mushroom cultivation on agricultural waste, affordable growing rooms and a simple starter equipment list.

Will it guarantee profit?

No honest guide can. It gives you the costing, record-keeping and pricing tools to see your real numbers and decide.

What format is it?

An 83-page colour PDF, readable on phone, tablet, computer or printed.
